# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Club and underground venue-goers aged 20–35. Late nights, intimate rooms, medium venues. They discover music through personal recommendation, not algorithm. They value authenticity over spectacle.

## Product Purpose
Saint Dizzie is a DJ brand identity. The logo is the primary touchpoint — flyers, social media, merchandise, digital profiles. Success means the logo reads as immediately distinctive, works at any size, and earns credibility in both club and underground scenes.

## Brand Personality
Sanctified. Slick. Spare.

## Anti-references
Generic EDM neon aesthetics — multiple glowing rings, laser grids, cyan/pink neon combos. Every festival poster from 2016–2022. Looks like a template.

## Design Principles
- **Restraint over spectacle**: The confidence is in what's left out, not what's added.
- **The "Saint" as rank**: The title should feel earned and weighty, not decorative.
- **Merch-first**: Every decision must survive being printed on black and white fabric at small sizes.
- **Underground credibility**: Never look like it was designed by committee or AI defaults.
- **Streetwear precision**: Bold type, deliberate geometry, graphic clarity — could live on a drop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
High contrast required. The logo must hold legibility at small sizes on dark backgrounds (club flyers, phone lock screens). Two-colorway system (dark/light) maintained for flexibility.
